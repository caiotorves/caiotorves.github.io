ML · Forecasting

Ibovespa Trend Forecasting

Time Series · Classification Model · FIAP Tech Challenge

Developed a machine learning classification model (Up/Down) to predict significant daily movements of the Brazilian stock market index. Using 5 years of IBOVESPA data (1,247 trading sessions), the project tackled market noise through threshold filtering and feature engineering — achieving 76.67% accuracy with a Random Forest model, surpassing the 75% challenge target.

EDA · Public Health

COVID-19 Data Analytics

PNAD-COVID-19 · Socioeconomic & Clinical Analysis · FIAP Tech Challenge

Analysis of Brazil's PNAD-COVID-19 survey data using PySpark on Databricks, following a Medallion architecture (Bronze → Silver → Gold layers) on Google Cloud Storage. Covered clinical symptom profiling, hospitalization probability, demographic analysis, geographic heat mapping, and the socioeconomic impact of the Emergency Aid (Auxílio Emergencial) — delivering strategic insights for healthcare resource allocation.
