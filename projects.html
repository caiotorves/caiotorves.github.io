Gov · Business Licensing

Regin

Business Registration Platform · JUCEB + SEBRAE + SEFAZ Integration

End-to-end digital transformation of the business registration process in the State of Bahia. What was once a fully manual, email-and-paper workflow became a self-service platform — integrated with the State Commercial Registry (JUCEB), SEBRAE, and the State Revenue Secretariat (SEFAZ) for in-platform payments. Citizens could open their own process in real time, with no waiting and no intermediaries.

  • Reduced business licensing time from 60+ days to real-time self-service
  • Played a key role in enabling a +53% increase in new business registrations
  • Enabled 34K+ businesses to be created in a single quarter
  • Designed all business rules, system workflows, and financial integrations with SEFAZ (DAE validation), eliminating manual document handling and wait cycles
  • Eliminated critical bureaucratic bottlenecks, significantly improving the ease of doing business and accelerating economic activity

Gov · Fire Safety

Fênix

Fire Safety Compliance Platform · Statewide + National Reach

Modernized the entire fire safety project submission and approval workflow — transitioning 100% of paper-based operations to a governed digital platform used by engineers, architects, and businesses across the entire state and from other parts of Brazil. Designed the validation logic, anti-fraud rules, and document workflow that defined how thousands of technical projects were reviewed and approved.

  • 90% reduction in project analysis rework through a document-locking mechanism — allowing analysts to approve correct files individually, removing the need for full reanalysis
  • 60%+ increase in analyst productivity by eliminating redundant review cycles
  • Designed anti-fraud validation rules preventing submission of fraudulent or incomplete technical files
  • Delivered a system used across the entire state by engineers, architects, and businesses — and by professionals from other parts of Brazil
  • Built executive dashboards for leadership to track team productivity, process velocity, project volume, and compliance KPIs
